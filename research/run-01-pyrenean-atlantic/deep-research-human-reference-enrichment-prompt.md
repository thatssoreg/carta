10. SOURCE FITNESS
==================================================

Use CARTA's source-fitness policy.

Do not create an unrealistic primary-source purity test.

Importer/distributor/trade sources can be excellent substantive evidence for producer-world claims such as:

- biography;
- work history;
- farming;
- cellar practice;
- fruit sourcing;
- cuvée details;
- producer relationships;
- importer/access information.

Evaluate the SOURCE AGAINST THE CLAIM.

Examples:

- INAO / EU regulation for appellation law: excellent
- Plantgrape/VIVC/scientific paper for genetics: excellent
- importer with long producer relationship for cellar practice: potentially excellent
- retailer for current price/stock observation: potentially good
- retailer for genetic parentage: poor
- forum for an allocation sighting: useful lead/market observation
- forum for historical fact: inadequate without corroboration

Corroborate high-risk claims when needed.

Preserve contradictions.
