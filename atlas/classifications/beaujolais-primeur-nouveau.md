# Beaujolais primeur / nouveau

**A legal mention and a dated production history—not the whole region**

Under the current Beaujolais specification, the **primeur** or **nouveau** mention is available to red and rosé wines, not white wines.

## Historical anchors

The specification’s historical account distinguishes early-release trade before 1951 from the regulatory turn on **13 November 1951**. It records the third-Thursday release rule from **1985** and a mid-1980s production peak near 500,000 hectolitres.

The same source states that primeur never exceeded half of total Beaujolais production. CARTA therefore rejects any account that makes Nouveau synonymous with all Beaujolais.

## Scope discipline

Legal timing, historic production volume and later market reputation are different claims. A future Atlas lens may explore that history, but this canonical baseline does not write the finished editorial Then/Now composition in advance.
