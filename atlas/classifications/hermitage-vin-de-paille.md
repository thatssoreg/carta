# Hermitage vin de paille mention

**A governed mention, not a separate appellation**

White [Hermitage](../countries/france/appellations/hermitage.md) may additionally carry the *vin de paille* mention. The specification sets the terms precisely: grapes dried for at least 45 days between harvest and pressing, a minimum natural richness of 170 grams of sugar per litre — about 19.5% potential alcohol — and a yield of 15 hectolitres per hectare.

The grapes are [Marsanne](../grapes/marsanne.md) and [Roussanne](../grapes/roussanne.md), the same pair that makes still white Hermitage.

CARTA models this as a mention inside the Hermitage specification rather than as a second appellation. It is a third legal wine form on one hill, and one of the reasons the corridor cannot be read as a list of red-wine names.

Read the mention through [Northern Rhône](../countries/france/regions/northern-rhone.md).
