# Domaine Christiane Chambeyron-Manin

**Ampuis, Côte-Rôtie, France**

Domaine Christiane Chambeyron-Manin is a tiny Côte-Rôtie estate centered on roughly half a hectare of Syrah on Côte Brune. Its scale is small, but the estate is a useful reference because the available evidence is unusually specific about site, cellar, and current stewardship.

## Background & trajectory

Current importer material describes the domaine as a multigenerational family estate now owned and run by **Véronique Manin**. The Wine Trust identifies Christiane as Véronique's mother and explains the domaine name through the family's maternal and paternal names.

CARTA currently has no accepted evidence linking this estate genealogically to other Chambeyron or Levet domaines, so shared surname or proximity does not become lineage.

## Place & vineyards

The domaine farms a single roughly **0.5-hectare parcel on Côte Brune**, above Ampuis. Sacred Thirst describes a very steep site on iron-rich schist where all vineyard work is done by hand.

The exact parcel polygon is not yet known. CARTA therefore represents Côte Brune as a named locality/site within the Côte-Rôtie appellation rather than fabricating a surveyed vineyard boundary.

## Farming

Importer descriptions characterize the farming as sustainable and organic-method oriented. CARTA keeps that wording narrower than a formal certification claim because no certification record was captured.

## In the cellar

The most detailed current technical description comes from Sacred Thirst:

- whole-cluster fermentation
- native yeasts
- concrete tanks for fermentation
- traditional basket pressing
- gravity transfer to 500-liter oak barrels
- up to one-third new oak depending on vintage
- about 18 months of élevage
- unfined and unfiltered bottling

CARTA therefore records **concrete fermentation followed by oak élevage**, not “concrete aging.”

## Grapes

The parcel is described as exclusively **Syrah**. Sacred Thirst uses the phrase “Serine clone of syrah.”

CARTA keeps one grape identity, `grape:syrah`, and records **Sérine** as local selection/clone terminology in this context. It does not create a separate Sérine grape entity.

## Wine to know

**Chambeyron-Manin Côte-Rôtie** is the estate's singular current CARTA wine anchor. It is modeled as one durable wine identity rather than as separate entities by vintage.

## People, lineage & influence

**Véronique Manin** is the current proprietor/winemaker in the accepted source set.

**Christiane Chambeyron-Manin** is retained as the maternal namesake and family node directly supported by current importer material.

Other Chambeyron and Levet family relationships remain research questions. Shared surname or proximity is not enough for CARTA to create lineage.

## Style & significance

Chambeyron-Manin gives CARTA a high-resolution micro-estate example in Côte-Rôtie: one tiny site, one grape, whole-cluster native fermentation, and a cellar regime that is specific enough to document without pretending every vintage is identical.

Its importance here does not depend on production scale or fame.

## Appellation context

INAO recognizes **Côte-Rôtie** as an AOC and maintains the current specification. CARTA has not yet ingested the official appellation polygon or the Chambeyron-Manin parcel geometry.

## What CARTA does not claim

The accepted evidence does **not** establish that these vines are pre-phylloxera or own-rooted. Current sources only support a roughly 40-to-50-plus-year vine-age range.

CARTA also does not currently claim that Domaine Christiane Chambeyron-Manin is related or unrelated to every other domaine carrying the Chambeyron name. That question remains unresolved rather than being converted into a confident negative genealogy.
