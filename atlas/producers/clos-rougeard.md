# Clos Rougeard

**Chacé, Saumur — family history, ownership change, and plural wine routes**

Clos Rougeard is CARTA’s central Loire door into the Foucault world, Saumur-Champigny Cabernet Franc, Saumur Chenin, and the difference between family lineage and current ownership.

## Family history and ownership

The estate’s current first-party account traces its history to 1664 and eight family generations. It identifies Jean-Louis “Charly” Foucault and Bernard “Nady” Foucault as the latest family generation at its head, then records the Bouygues family’s acquisition of the estate in 2017.

CARTA preserves both eras. It does not describe current ownership as an unbroken Foucault family estate, nor erase the family history because ownership changed.

## Red and white routes

The current range keeps Cabernet Franc wines in the Saumur-Champigny route distinct from Brézé, its Chenin wine. Clos Rougeard is therefore a door into both red and white Saumur worlds, not evidence for one regional style.

The wider ecology includes an evidenced work and family line: Antoine Foucault, Jean-Louis Foucault’s son, worked with his father and uncle at Clos Rougeard before establishing Domaine du Collier with Caroline Boireau in 1999. That lineage does not make the two estates identical.

## Map and evidence boundary
