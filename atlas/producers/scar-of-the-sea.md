# Scar of the Sea / Bassi Vineyard

**Avila Valley, San Luis Obispo Coast, California**

Scar of the Sea is a San Luis Obispo wine project established in 2012 and currently owned and operated by **Mikey and Gina Giugni**. The cellar seed **Bassi Vineyard Chardonnay** opens a CARTA world where a purchased-fruit relationship became direct farming and vineyard ownership over time.

## Producer and people

Scar of the Sea states that Mikey and Gina Giugni own and operate the project.

Independent reporting on the 2024 Bassi acquisition identifies Mikey and Gina as majority owners of the vineyard, with **Will and Coral Frischkorn** as partners.

## Bassi Vineyard

Scar of the Sea says it began fully farming Bassi in March 2024 and purchased the property in July 2024.

The producer describes Bassi as:

- in the hillsides of **Avila Valley**;
- about 1.2 miles from Avila Beach;
- due south facing;
- on sandstone;
- within the **San Luis Obispo Coast / SLO Coast AVA**.

Bassi received **Demeter certification in July 2024** after the Giugnis completed the final year of conversion.

The ownership chronology matters. Scar had been buying Bassi fruit since 2019 before the 2024 acquisition. CARTA therefore does not flatten fruit purchase, farming and ownership into one timeless relationship.

## Bassi Vineyard Chardonnay

CARTA treats Bassi Vineyard Chardonnay as one durable wine identity across vintages.

Scar's trade page currently lists the **2023 Bassi Vineyard Chardonnay, SLO Coast**. Specialist importer technical material describes that release as:

- 100% Chardonnay;
- whole-cluster pressed;
- native-yeast fermentation in neutral French oak;
- natural malolactic fermentation;
- 19 months on lees;
- 10% from a Bassi Chardonnay solera begun in 2019;
- unfined and unfiltered;
- minimal sulfur.

These are 2023-scoped cellar facts.
