## Le Haut-Lieu

Huet describes Le Haut-Lieu as its historic vineyard, acquired in 1928. The producer currently gives the site as approximately 15 hectares on the Vouvray plateau and describes very deep brown-clay soil.

Le Haut-Lieu is a vineyard identity. It is not the same entity as the wine that carries its name.
