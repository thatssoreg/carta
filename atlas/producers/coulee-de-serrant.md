# La Coulée de Serrant

**Savennières — estate, appellation, and advocacy network kept distinct**

La Coulée de Serrant is CARTA’s Anjou door into Chenin, the Savennières family of legal origins, a dated estate biodynamic chronology, and the later Renaissance des Appellations network. These are related records, not interchangeable names.

## Estate chronology

The estate’s first-party history places Nicolas Joly there from 1976, his encounter with biodynamics in 1980, and conversion of the full vineyard by 1984. These dates belong to this estate; they do not establish a regional rule or an invention claim.

Renaissance des Appellations was formed in 2001 with Joly as its founding organizer. CARTA models it as a biodynamic advocacy and membership network, not as a synonym for the Loire, natural wine, or every producer that shares some practices.

## Three legal identities

Savennières, Savennières Roche aux Moines, and Coulée de Serrant retain separate current denomination identities in INAO geography. Their common Chenin route does not collapse them into one polygon or one producer story. The producer named La Coulée de Serrant and the Coulée de Serrant AOP are likewise separate entities.
