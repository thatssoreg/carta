# Soleras del Pacífico

> **Navigation node:** this project, its representative wine and Evangelho Vineyard are composed into one honest stub.

## Overview

Soleras del Pacífico is Jake Neustadt's project, begun after he gained access in 2017 to old Palomino vines at Evangelho Vineyard while working as a Bedrock Wine Co. viticulturist.

## The Flor of Evangelho

The represented wine is a single-vineyard Palomino Fino identity. Accepted authority documents native barrel fermentation, light spring fortification, flor aging, fractional blending through a four-level solera and unfiltered bottling.

Evangelho is represented as a Contra Costa County vineyard owned and farmed by Bedrock. CARTA keeps the Californian flor/solera practice connected to its source without declaring it equivalent to Jura sous-voile traditions.
