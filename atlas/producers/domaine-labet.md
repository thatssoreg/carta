# Domaine Labet

> **Navigation node:** this is an evidence-backed orientation, not yet a full baseline producer profile.

## Overview

Domaine Labet is a family estate based in Rotalier in the southern Jura. The accepted interprofessional source documents 13.5 hectares across 45 parcels in four communes and identifies Alain, Julien, Charline and Romain Labet among the family vignerons.

## Wines and cellar anchors

- **Fleur de Savagnin** is represented as a multi-parcel Savagnin wine rather than a single-vineyard identity.
- **Chardonnay du Hasard / La Cuvée du Hasard** is represented as old-vine Chardonnay assembled from several Lias-marl terroirs and aged sous voile in old 228-litre pièces.
- **La Bardette Chardonnay** remains a persistent wine identity with the accepted parcel and élevage details scoped to the 2022 vintage.

CARTA keeps ouillage and sous-voile aging as distinct practices. It rejects the unsupported transfer of fortification into the Chardonnay du Hasard account and does not generalize malolactic avoidance across the estate.

## Depth status

A baseline promotion still requires a broader estate history, farming account, portfolio orientation, style synthesis and access pass.
