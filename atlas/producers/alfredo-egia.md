# Alfredo Egia

**Balmaseda, Bizkaia, Spain**

Alfredo Egia is a Basque grower and winemaker working around Balmaseda in Bizkaia. The first CARTA research connects his work to Petit Manseng, Petit Courbu, a close professional relationship with Imanol Garay, and wines that push well beyond the light, brisk Txakoli stereotype while remaining rooted in the region's grapes and legal context.

## Background & trajectory

The accepted Run 01 evidence documents Imanol Garay as an important mentor to Egia. A fuller biography, career chronology, and account of how Egia arrived at his current farming and cellar approach are priorities for the next enrichment pass.

## Place & vineyards

Egia's producer work is associated with vineyards in and around Balmaseda, within Bizkaia and the Bizkaiko Txakolina context.

Exact parcels, tenure, elevation, aspect, geology, and hydrology have not yet been normalized into CARTA. The current spatial record is intentionally municipality-level rather than a fabricated vineyard point.

## Cellar approach

Importer and specialist reporting on **Rebel Rebel** documents whole-cluster direct pressing, spontaneous fermentation in barrel and amphora, extended lees aging, some intentionally untopped élevage, and very low or no added sulfur depending on vintage.

CARTA does not treat one wine or vintage as a permanent recipe for the producer. The enrichment pass should establish which practices recur across the range and which are cuvée- or vintage-specific.
