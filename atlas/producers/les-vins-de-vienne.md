# Les Vins de Vienne

**Seyssuel · a replanted vineyard that is not a ninth cru**

Les Vins de Vienne is a project identity, distinct from the domaines of the three growers who started it. The Syndicat des Vignerons de l'AOC Condrieu records [Yves Cuilleron](domaine-yves-cuilleron.md), Pierre Gaillard and François Villard beginning the project in 1996 to revive the old Seyssuel vineyard north of Vienne, producing a first vintage in 1998, and farming twelve hectares of Seyssuel hillside planted to [Syrah](../grapes/syrah.md) and [Viognier](../grapes/viognier.md).

## The point of the case

Sotanum, with Héluicum and Taburnum, is sold as [Collines Rhodaniennes IGP](../countries/france/appellations/collines-rhodaniennes.md). Seyssuel sits in the same corridor as the crus, on comparable slopes, farmed by three cru producers — and its wines are still not cru wines, because no cru appellation covers that ground.

Movement on the map between Seyssuel and [Côte-Rôtie](../countries/france/appellations/cote-rotie.md) is a few kilometres. Movement in legal category is total. That is the whole lesson, and it is why CARTA treats Seyssuel as a regional frontier rather than quietly adding a ninth cru.

## What is kept separate

Three people each founded one project; the project farms in a place; its wines are classified as an IGP; and the founders' own estates remain their own producer identities. CARTA holds four different relationships there rather than one.

Read the project through [Northern Rhône](../countries/france/regions/northern-rhone.md).
