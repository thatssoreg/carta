# Domaine Georges Vernay

**Condrieu · four generations across a near-disappearance**

Domaine Georges Vernay is the strongest single route into [Condrieu](../countries/france/appellations/condrieu.md)'s twentieth century — and the best place to see why "he saved Viognier" is useful shorthand and poor factual authority.

## The chronology the estate gives

Francis Vernay created the estate at Condrieu and planted its first vines at the [Coteau de Vernon](../countries/france/appellations/condrieu.md), releasing a first Coteau de Vernon cuvée in 1940. Georges Vernay took over from his father in 1953. Christine Vernay took over in 1996, added the Terrasses de l'Empire and the estate's first red cuvées, finished the reconstruction of the Coteau de Vernon and converted the whole domaine to organic growing. The range spans [Côte-Rôtie](../countries/france/appellations/cote-rotie.md), Condrieu and [Saint-Joseph](../countries/france/appellations/saint-joseph.md).

## What "saved" can and cannot carry

The Syndicat des Vignerons de l'AOC Condrieu describes Georges Vernay as a man of vision and conviction who "worked hard to rebuild the hillsides and rescue the Viognier grape variety from obscurity", earning the nickname of the Pope of Condrieu. The estate's own history says he is globally recognised as having saved the abandoned variety, replanted his land and revitalised a generation of growers.

CARTA carries the documented leading role. It does not promote the shorthand into sole causation, because the same recovery contains Francis Vernay's earlier planting, [André Perret](domaine-andre-perret.md) returning in the 1980s specifically to plant and develop the AOC, [Yves Cuilleron](domaine-yves-cuilleron.md) taking over 3.5 hectares in 1987, and [E. Guigal](e-guigal.md) putting Condrieu in front of a much larger market.

## Open questions

The estate's current generation beyond Christine Vernay is reported in trade material that CARTA has not accepted, so it is left out rather than dated loosely. A dated hectare series for Condrieu would let the recovery be shown as a curve rather than two endpoints. And no producer base coordinate has been acquired from a fit source, so the domaine carries no map point.
