# Arnot-Roberts

**California, United States**

Arnot-Roberts was founded in Healdsburg in 2001 by childhood friends Duncan Arnot Meyers and Nathan Lee Roberts. The cellar seed **Clary Ranch Syrah** opens a useful CARTA world around a grower-owned vineyard, Petaluma Gap climate, Syrah, and the distinction between producer identity and vineyard identity.

## Producer and people

Arnot-Roberts' current history identifies Duncan Arnot Meyers and Nathan Lee Roberts as co-founders. The same first-party history says Caitlin Quinn joined as assistant winemaker in 2015 and specifically identifies Syrah as central to her interest in the winery.

## Clary Ranch

Arnot-Roberts says Paul Clary planted Pinot Noir and Syrah at Clary Ranch in 2000. The ranch is currently described as owned and tended by Drew Beuchley and the crew that has worked the vines since planting.

The producer places the vineyard in the heart of the Petaluma Gap near Tomales Bay and gives:

- elevation: 300 feet;
- Syrah clone/selection: 470;
- soil: Steinbeck-series sedimentary clay loam;
- farming: organic;
- planting year: 2000.

The producer also says the site can be farmed without irrigation.
