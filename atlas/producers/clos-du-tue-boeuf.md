# Clos du Tue-Boeuf

> **Navigation node:** this is an evidence-backed estate orientation, not yet a full baseline producer profile.

## Overview

Clos du Tue-Boeuf is a Loire estate in Les Montils associated in the accepted trade record with Thierry and Zoë Puzelat. The same source records 14 hectares plus purchased fruit and certified-organic farming.

## Le Brin de Chèvre

The represented 2024 Le Brin de Chèvre is a Vin de France white composed of 90% Menu Pineau and 10% Petit Meslier from neighboring parcels planted in 1937 and 1950, with later massale-selection replantings contributing part of the wine. Those dates do not support calling the vines pre-phylloxera.

Menu Pineau is connected to its supported local Orbois name through governed name authority. Petit Meslier remains a vintage-scoped component here rather than an automatic standalone page.

## Law and place

Vin de France is rendered as a classification, not an appellation or physical container. The Loire Valley and Les Montils remain geographic relationships, so the page does not imply that a Vin de France label is a Loire appellation.
