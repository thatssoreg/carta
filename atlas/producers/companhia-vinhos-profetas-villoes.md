# Companhia de Vinhos dos Profetas e dos Villões

> **Navigation node:** this is an evidence-backed project orientation, not yet a full baseline producer profile.

## Overview

Companhia de Vinhos dos Profetas e dos Villões is the Madeira and Porto Santo project of António Maçanita and Nuno Faria. The first-party chronology places the beginning of the collaboration around the start of the pandemic rather than at an invented exact founding date.

## Porto Santo wines

- **Listrão dos Profetas 2022** is represented as an old-vine Porto Santo white made from Listrão and released under D.O. Madeirense.
- **Vinho da Corda dos Profetas 2023** is represented as a Porto Santo wine from Listrão and Caracol, using the richer final press fraction associated with the historical vinho da corda method.

Listrão is governed as a supported local name on the existing Palomino Fino identity. Caracol remains a separate unresolved grape identity; CARTA does not merge it with Palomino/Listán or another grape without stronger ampelographic authority.

## Law and place

DOP Madeirense and fortified DOP Madeira are distinct legal designations. The represented still wines are classified as Madeirense; the navigation system keeps that legal relationship separate from physical Porto Santo and Madeira Archipelago geography.

## Depth status

Baseline promotion still requires a broader project, vineyard, farming, cellar, portfolio, style and access pass.
