# Domaines Landron

**La Haie-Fouassière, Pays Nantais — continuity with dated change**

Domaines Landron is CARTA’s western producer door into Muscadet, Muscadet Sèvre et Maine, and Melon de Bourgogne. The estate history is valuable because it supplies a chronology rather than a timeless identity claim.

## Estate chronology

The domaine records Jo Landron joining his father in 1979. It dates the abandonment of herbicides and return to soil cultivation to 1987, the beginning of organic conversion to 1999, Ecocert certification to 2002, and entry into Biodyvin certification to 2011.

Those dates describe one estate. CARTA does not make them an origin myth for Muscadet, a regional rule, or proof of any wine’s cellar protocol.

## Place and rules

Muscadet Sèvre et Maine is a distinct current AOP in the Nantais route, centered on Melon de Bourgogne. The legal `sur lie` mention is not a synonym for the appellation and does not mean every long lees-aged wine qualifies for it.

The Atlas point uses the domaine’s published base at 3 impasse du Fief du Breil in La Haie-Fouassière. It is a production/business base, not a parcel or holdings map.

## Evidence boundary

The current profile establishes history, base, regional context, and a governed farming chronology. Individual cuvées, parcels, élevage, sulfur, filtration, and current access remain wine- and vintage-specific research rather than inferred estate rules.
