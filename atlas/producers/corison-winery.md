# Corison Winery

> **Navigation node:** this is an evidence-backed producer orientation, not yet a full baseline profile.

## Overview

Cathy Corison founded Corison Winery and began making Cabernet Sauvignon under her own name in 1987. CARTA keeps the continuing Napa Valley Cabernet identity separate from the Kronos and Sunbasket single-vineyard wines.

## Vineyards and wines

- **Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon** is represented as a continuing multi-vineyard wine sourced from three benchland vineyards between Rutherford and St. Helena.
- **Kronos Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon** is tied to Kronos, planted in 1971 on St. George rootstock and purchased by Cathy Corison and William Martin in 1995.
- **Sunbasket Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon** is tied to Sunbasket, purchased in 2015 after more than 25 years of fruit sourcing.

St. Helena AVA remains a legal geography distinct from the winery's physical location and from the broader Napa Valley designation. Its accepted establishment date is September 11, 1995.
