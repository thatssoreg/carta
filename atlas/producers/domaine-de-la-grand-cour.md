# Domaine de la Grand’Cour

**Fleurie, Beaujolais, France**

Domaine de la Grand’Cour’s first-party history says Jean Dutraive acquired the estate in **1969**, with nine hectares in Fleurie across **Grand’Cour**, **Chapelle des Bois** and **Champagne**. Jean-Louis Dutraive joined in 1977 and succeeded him in 1989.

## Sites without invented shapes

The producer also documents Brouilly holdings. Estate fruit remains distinct from the family’s later purchased-fruit work after severe hail in 2016 and 2017.

## Producer-described cellar practice

The domaine describes hand-harvested whole bunches put into tank without crushing or destemming, at low temperature and saturated with carbon dioxide. It describes spontaneous fermentation through pressing, no added sulfite and no oenological products except intervention if a problem occurs.

This supports producer-specific carbonic and whole-cluster relationships. It is not a universal Fleurie, Brouilly or Beaujolais rule.
