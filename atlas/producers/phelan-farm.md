# Phelan Farm

**Cambria, California, United States**

Phelan Farm is a coastal California vineyard and wine project in Cambria whose present identity grew from a 2007 planting by Greg Phelan and a later collaboration with Raj Parr. The farm is a useful CARTA reference because it brings cool-climate European varieties, including Mondeuse, into the recently established San Luis Obispo Coast AVA while also testing how persistent wine identities should hold changing vintage-level cellar practice.

## Background & trajectory

Greg Phelan planted several acres of Chardonnay and Pinot Noir on their own roots at the Cambria property in 2007. Importer documentation places Raj Parr's introduction to Phelan in 2017, after which the two worked together on vineyard redevelopment, plant material, and a wider set of cool-climate varieties.

CARTA therefore keeps Greg Phelan, Raj Parr, and Phelan Farm as separate identities. Current evidence supports a working relationship, but it does not justify collapsing Parr's other projects into Phelan Farm or treating Parr as the farm's original founder.

## Place & vineyard

Phelan Farm sits in Cambria on California's cool Pacific coast. The San Luis Obispo Coast, also called SLO Coast, became an American Viticultural Area in 2022. First-party vineyard material emphasizes the maritime setting and maps a diverse set of grapes across named vineyard sections.

Mondeuse is currently listed in the Jensen and Devine sections. Parcel geometry and the exact tenure structure remain future CARTA work rather than inferred facts.

## Farming

Phelan Farm describes no-till farming since 2007, livestock integration, natural farm-made preparations, and the avoidance of synthetic herbicides and fungicides. The producer also states that sulfur and copper are not used on the property.

These statements are recorded as current producer-described practice, not as a timeless certification claim.

## In the cellar

The producer describes spontaneous fermentation with natural yeast, older oak for maturation, no sulfur in vineyard or cellar, and hand bottling without fining or filtration.

The exact stem protocol for the Mondeuse is unresolved. A 2021 technical sheet describes whole-cluster fermentation, while a current importer description says the single-parcel Mondeuse is destemmed. CARTA preserves that contradiction instead of inventing a clean chronology.

## Wine to know

**Phelan Farm Mondeuse** is the seed wine for this reference. Current sources support it as a varietal Mondeuse wine. It remains one durable CARTA wine identity across vintages, while stem use and other vintage-specific cellar choices belong in dated claims.

The Run 03D research report accidentally imported language from the earlier Hiyu/Moon Hill work, including a Columbia Gorge reference and solera-like framing. Those details do not belong to Phelan Farm and were not ingested.

## People & relationships

- **Greg Phelan** planted the original Chardonnay and Pinot Noir in 2007.
- **Raj Parr** began working with Phelan after their 2017 introduction.

Parr's relationships to Sandhi, Domaine de la Côte, Evening Land, and other projects are real research opportunities, but they should be modeled only when those worlds are independently reconciled.

### Bassi Vineyard connection

Run 03G's Scar of the Sea research initially treated Phelan Farm and Bassi Vineyard as sharing only SLO Coast geography. Reconciliation found more.

Phelan Farm's own wines page lists **Brij Bassi Syrah 2021** among purchased-grape wines. Independent 2024 reporting also says Raj Parr agreed to provide **Pink Chardonnay cuttings from Phelan Farm** for Bassi replanting after Mikey and Gina Giugni acquired the vineyard.

CARTA preserves these as direct research leads without over-modeling them. `Brij` still needs independent identity resolution, and an announced cutting transfer is not encoded as completed propagation until a later source confirms it occurred.
