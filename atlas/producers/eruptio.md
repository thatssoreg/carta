## Arinto dos Açores

The represented 2020 Eruptio Arinto dos Açores is a Pico D.O. wine. Its vintage-scoped authority records Arinto dos Açores, French-oak fermentation and six months on fine lees.

CARTA keeps Arinto dos Açores biologically distinct from mainland Arinto. Portugal's official variety list recognizes Terrantez da Terceira as its synonym while listing mainland Arinto separately with Pedernã.
