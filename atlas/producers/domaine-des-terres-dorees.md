# Domaine des Terres Dorées

**Charnay, southern Beaujolais, France**

Domaine des Terres Dorées supplies a southern Beaujolais counterweight to a cru-only regional story. The current public record places the domaine in Charnay and describes activity around the Terres Dorées as well as Fleurie, Moulin-à-Vent, Brouilly and Morgon.

## The 2024 transition

In September 2024, Domaines Roger Zannier announced that Jean-Paul Brun had entrusted operation and development of the domaine to the group from the 2024 harvest. The same announcement says Brun would accompany a five-year transition focused on making the wines.

CARTA records bounded `WORKED_WITH` relationships. It does **not** infer an asset sale, a completed retirement or an ownership transfer beyond the sources’ wording.

## Quantities held back

Rhône Tourisme currently gives a 57-hectare headline but describes 37 hectares around the domaine plus 18 hectares in the crus. Because 37 + 18 does not equal 57, CARTA records the mismatch as an open question and publishes no manufactured total.
