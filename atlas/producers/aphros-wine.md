# Aphros Wine / Phaunus

**Lima Valley, Portugal**

Aphros Wine is Vasco Croft's wine project rooted in the Lima Valley. The cellar seed **Phaunus Loureiro** resolves Phaunus as part of the Aphros portfolio and opens a CARTA world around Loureiro, amphora fermentation, biodynamic practice, Lima Valley, and an unresolved question about the exact legal designation of the Phaunus bottling.

## Producer and project

Aphros's first-party history places the beginning of the modern project at Casal do Paço in the Lima Valley. Vasco Croft took responsibility for restructuring the property in 2004 and developed it around biodynamic ideas.

Aphros later expanded into additional properties near Ponte de Lima. Its current wine portfolio lists several `Phaunus` bottlings, including Phaunus Loureiro, Phaunus Palhete and Phaunus Pet Nat.

CARTA therefore models:

- Aphros Wine as the producer;
- Vasco Croft as the central person;
- Phaunus as an Aphros wine line/project;
- Phaunus Loureiro as a separate durable wine identity.

Phaunus is not treated as a separate estate.

## Phaunus Loureiro

The current first-party 2023 technical page identifies:

- producer: Vasco Croft / Aphros Wine Lda.;
- oenologues: Tiago Sampaio and Miguel Viseu;
- region: Northwest Portugal;
- grape: 100% Loureiro;
- spontaneous fermentation with skins in clay amphorae lined with beeswax;
- seven months of sur-lies aging;
- total sulfur: 30 mg/L.

Those cellar details are recorded as **2023-specific**, not timeless rules for every vintage.

This corrects Run 03F-A's internal conflict between three months, six months, and seven months of amphora/skin/lees time.

## Farming and sulfur discipline

Aphros describes biodynamic preparations, cover crops, animals, and other biodynamic practices in its project history.

Its cellar-practice page explicitly says SO2 use is not universally zero. Aphros describes reducing sulfur to low levels but says its protocol can use up to 25 ppm free SO2 at bottling and 0–50 ppm total SO2 depending on the wine.

CARTA therefore rejects the report's universal “no added sulfur” characterization.

No third-party biodynamic certification claim was necessary for the baseline profile.

## Geography and legal designation

Aphros places its foundational and expanded properties in the Lima Valley and near Ponte de Lima, with extensive Loureiro plantings and granitic sites.

However, the current first-party **Phaunus Loureiro 2023** page says only `Northwest of Portugal`.

Other Aphros wines explicitly say `Vinho Verde DOC – Sub-Region of Lima`, but that wording does not automatically transfer to Phaunus Loureiro.

CARTA therefore keeps the precise legal appellation of Phaunus Loureiro **provisional/unresolved** until the bottle label or an explicit first-party source confirms it.

This is an important example of refusing cross-wine contamination inside the same producer portfolio.
