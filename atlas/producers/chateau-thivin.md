# Château Thivin

**Mont Brouilly, Odenas, Beaujolais, France**

Château Thivin’s first-party history dates the Geoffray family’s tenure from **1877** and presents a continuing family domaine focused principally on **Côte de Brouilly** and **Brouilly**.

## A hill and two legal areas

Mont Brouilly is a physical hill. Côte de Brouilly occupies slopes on it; Brouilly is a separate, broader surrounding cru area. The two INAO shapes represent regulatory geographical areas, not estate vineyards, and the hill is not itself an appellation.
