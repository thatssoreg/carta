# JONATA

> **Navigation node:** this is an evidence-backed estate orientation, not yet a full baseline producer profile.

## Overview

JONATA was established in 1998 when a group acquired the 600-acre property. E. Stanley Kroenke later invested and became sole owner in 2008, while Matt Dees was recruited for the first vintage in 2004. CARTA rejects the weaker chronology that placed the acquisition in 2000 or made Kroenke sole owner from the beginning.

## Place and farming

The estate vineyard is represented in Ballard Canyon AVA, within Santa Ynez Valley and Santa Barbara County, California. JONATA states that the estate vineyard became officially certified organic in 2022.

Ballard Canyon is an AVA established October 2, 2013. Santa Barbara County is an administrative/county appellation context, not itself an AVA; the projection keeps those semantic types distinct.

## Todos

Todos remains one persistent wine identity whose estate blend changes with vineyard and vintage. The represented 2022 composition is temporal authority: 70% Syrah, 15% Petite Sirah, 10% Cabernet Sauvignon, 3% Merlot and 2% miscellaneous red and white varieties, followed by 20 months in French oak. CARTA does not create a new wine identity for each recipe.
