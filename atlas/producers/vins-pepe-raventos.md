# Vins Pepe Raventós

> **Navigation node:** this project, vineyard and wine are composed into one honest reader-facing stub.

## Overview

Vins Pepe Raventós is Pepe Raventós's small personal wine project. The accepted first-party record describes parcel-based natural wines made at the Mas del Serral garage and dates the project from 2014.

## Bastard Negre

The represented 2022 wine comes from Les Terrasses del Serral, a west-side parcel planted in 1974, and was fermented spontaneously in open casks and one qvevri. CARTA does not call those vines pre-phylloxera.

The estate's Bastard Negre is provisionally connected to the Graciano identity through the reported Morastell DNA result and Spain's official Morrastel/Graciano synonym record. That resolution is explicitly local and provisional; it does not redefine every historical use of the name.

## Depth status

A baseline promotion still requires a broader project history, farming account, portfolio and style pass, plus stronger primary genetics evidence for the accession.
