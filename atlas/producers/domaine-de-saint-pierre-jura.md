# Domaine de Saint Pierre / Château Renard

**Mathenay, Jura, France**

Domaine de Saint Pierre is the Jura estate associated with **Fabrice Dodane**. The cellar seed **Château Renard** resolves to a recurring white Arbois bottling and opens a CARTA world around Mathenay, Arbois AOP, Chardonnay, Savagnin, organic farming, and the need to keep wine composition vintage-specific.

## Estate and people

Specialist and reference sources agree that Dodane joined the domaine as manager in 1989 after enology study and work in the Jura. They differ on whether his acquisition/takeover should be dated to 2011 or 2012, so CARTA records the succession without manufacturing a false exact date.

Ecocert currently lists Fabrice Dodane / Domaine de Saint-Pierre in Mathenay under EU organic certification. CARTA therefore accepts current certified-organic status while declining to force a disputed historical certification year into authority.

## Château Renard

Château Renard is modeled as one durable wine identity across vintages and as an Arbois wine made by Domaine de Saint Pierre.

The Run 03F-B report described the wine as permanently 100% Chardonnay. Reconciliation found that vintage evidence is not uniform:

- Beaune Imports identifies **2018 Château Renard** as Chardonnay, Arbois;
- iDealwine describes **2020 Château Renard** as Chardonnay with a small proportion of Savagnin.

CARTA therefore temporalizes composition rather than universalizing 100% Chardonnay across the wine's life.

## Arbois

INAO describes Arbois as an AOP for still white, red and rosé wines in the Jura and supplies the current legal specification and delimitation resources.

CARTA keeps the legal appellation, the Mathenay estate location, and the durable wine identity separate.

## Cellar restraint

The research report supplied multiple low-sulfur, no-sulfur, ouillé and barrel-aging statements from trade sources. Those can be valuable vintage or producer context, but the baseline authority does not turn them into timeless Château Renard rules.
