# Saint-Péray sparkling trials of 1828

> **Navigation node:** this honest stub keeps a meaningful CARTA subject discoverable without presenting it as a finished baseline reference.

The [Saint-Péray](../countries/france/appellations/saint-peray.md) specification records a merchant's cellarmaster experimenting with bottle fermentation in 1730, and Louis-Alexandre Faure carrying out systematic trials on a hundred barrels in 1828 — an innovation it describes as unique in the Rhône valley at the time. The appellation treats this as the origin of the [sparkling](../practices/traditional-method-sparkling.md) reputation it carried through the nineteenth century.

Trade accounts commonly date the first sparkling Saint-Péray to 1829. CARTA carries the specification's 1828 trials and does not merge the two dates.
