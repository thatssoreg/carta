# Bizkaia

Bizkaia is the Spanish administrative geography containing Balmaseda and the current legal landscape of Bizkaiko Txakolina represented in Run 01. It is distinct from broader cultural definitions of the Basque Country, which can cross current administrative and national boundaries.

## Overview

The first CARTA pilot enters Bizkaia through Alfredo Egia, Balmaseda, Bizkaiko Txakolina, and a cluster of grape-name relationships that connect Basque legal terminology to varieties also associated with the French side of the western Pyrenees.
