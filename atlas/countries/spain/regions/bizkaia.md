# Bizkaia

**Province of the Basque Country, Spain**

Bizkaia is a rugged Atlantic province around Bilbao whose wine identity is most visibly carried by Txakoli. The province is more than the [Bizkaiko Txakolina](../appellations/bizkaiko-txakolina.md) denomination, however. It contains coastal and inland growing environments, old Basque grape names, a revived wine culture, and contemporary producers testing how much stylistic range that material can support.

## Geography

Bizkaia rises quickly from the Bay of Biscay into steep, folded hills and valleys. Rainfall is high by Spanish standards, humidity matters, and vineyard success depends heavily on drainage, exposure, air movement, and the ability to ripen fruit in an Atlantic climate.

The CARTA pilot began in **Balmaseda**, inland and west of Bilbao, rather than in the most familiar postcard-coastal Txakoli zone. That difference matters. Bizkaia should not be reduced to sea spray and coastal pergolas.

## Wine law

The main legal wine object in the current reference is [Bizkaiko Txakolina](../appellations/bizkaiko-txakolina.md).

The DO's grape list is particularly important to CARTA because it preserves Basque legal names and connects them to grapes also known in France.

## Grapes

Key white material includes:

- [Courbu](../../../grapes/courbu.md) / Hondarrabi Zuri
- [Petit Courbu](../../../grapes/petit-courbu.md) / Hondarrabi Zuri Zerratia in current Bizkaiko law
- [Petit Manseng](../../../grapes/petit-manseng.md) / Izkiriota Ttipia
- [Gros Manseng](../../../grapes/gros-manseng.md)
- Folle Blanche and other authorized varieties outside the current core cards

An official 2008 regulation already listed Petit Courbu, Gros Manseng, and Petit Manseng among authorized varieties. Run 02's claim that all three were newly added only in 2020-21 was therefore rejected. The 2025 amendment is still important, especially for the current status and spelling of Hondarrabi Zuri Zerratia / Petit Courbu.

## Styles

The familiar Txakoli reference is dry, bright, relatively low in alcohol, and driven by Atlantic freshness. But Bizkaia also supports still, lees-aged, barrel-aged, and more textural expressions.

[Alfredo Egia](../../../producers/alfredo-egia.md) is particularly useful because his wines show how local grapes can support a broader structural range without requiring CARTA to redefine the whole region around one producer.

## Producers and projects

The next expansion should add a broader set of benchmark and contemporary Bizkaian producers so one network does not become a proxy for the province.

## History & significance

Txakoli's modern revival preserved and re-legitimized local wine culture after a long period of contraction. That revival also shaped the international frame: for many drinkers, “Txakoli” became synonymous with one bright, youthful style.

CARTA's interest is in keeping that familiar style visible while also showing the grapes, sites, and producers that make the category wider than the stereotype.
