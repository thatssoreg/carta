# Côte-Rôtie AOP

**The one red cru that lets a white grape in — and only at the grape stage**

Côte-Rôtie was recognised by the decree of 18 October 1940 and covers Ampuis, Saint-Cyr-sur-le-Rhône and Tupin-et-Semons on the right bank south of Vienne, where INAO records slopes steepening to 60% and terraces held by stone walls locally called *cheys*. The specification in force is the one homologated by the arrêté of 4 July 2024.

## Red only, and one unusual permission

Côte-Rôtie may only be a still red wine. [Syrah](../../../grapes/syrah.md) must be at least 80% of the holding's encépagement; [Viognier](../../../grapes/viognier.md) is the accessory variety, permitted in mixed plantings inside the Syrah parcels up to 20% of the vines. Where both are used, the specification requires that the wine be *vinifiés par assemblage des raisins concernés* — [co-fermented](../../../practices/co-fermentation.md) in the same proportion, not blended after the event.

That is a rule about when the two varieties may meet. It is not a description of anything a producer actually did: many Côte-Rôtie wines contain no Viognier at all, and CARTA does not read the permission as a practice.

## Côte Brune, and the limits of a famous pair

Côte Brune and Côte Blonde are powerful local vocabulary, not legal subzones. CARTA governs [Côte Brune](../../../landscapes/README.md) as a locality because [Domaine Christiane Chambeyron-Manin](../../../producers/domaine-christiane-chambeyron-manin.md) farms roughly half a hectare there, and stops short of drawing either as a polygon.

## A single appellation, several kinds of estate

The same name covers a half-hectare micro-estate, [Domaine Jamet](../../../producers/domaine-jamet.md) blending twenty-five plots across seventeen lieux-dits, [Jean-Michel Stéphan](../../../producers/jean-michel-stephan.md) working with no added sulfites since 1997 after a spell in Beaujolais, and [E. Guigal](../../../producers/e-guigal.md), a house founded in 1946 that has since bought several of its neighbours. Reading Côte-Rôtie as one kind of place would lose all four.
