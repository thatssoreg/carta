# Saint-Péray AOP

**The cru that is only white, and the only one with bubbles**

Saint-Péray was recognised by the decree of 8 December 1936. It covers the communes of Saint-Péray and Toulaud in the Ardèche, immediately south of [Cornas](cornas.md), and it makes both *vins blancs tranquilles* and *vins blancs mousseux* from [Marsanne](../../../grapes/marsanne.md) and [Roussanne](../../../grapes/roussanne.md). INAO gives sparkling wine as roughly a tenth of the volume.

Two neighbouring appellations, sharing a granite basement: one is red Syrah and nothing else, the other is white and may sparkle. Nothing in the ground explains that.

## Bubbles before reputation

The specification traces bottle-fermentation experiments to a merchant's cellarmaster in 1730 and records [Louis-Alexandre Faure conducting systematic trials on a hundred barrels in 1828](../../../historical-events/saint-peray-sparkling-1828.md), an innovation it describes as unique in the Rhône valley at the time. Trade accounts commonly date the first sparkling Saint-Péray to 1829; CARTA keeps the specification's 1828 trials and does not merge the two.

The sparkling wines are made by [second fermentation in bottle](../../../practices/traditional-method-sparkling.md).

## One estate holding both traditions

[Domaine Alain Voge](../../../producers/domaine-alain-voge.md) makes the time layer tangible. Its Les Bulles d'Alain is 100% Marsanne, méthode traditionnelle, brut nature, four years on lattes, presented as the continuation of the tradition that made the region's reputation from the 1830s. Its Ongrie cuvée was created in 1984, vinified only in 228- and 400-litre oak, at a moment when — in the estate's own words — most Saint-Péray came from the traditional method and still wines were made in tank.

That makes Voge a precursor of barrel work in Saint-Péray. It does not make him the inventor of still Saint-Péray, which the same passage says already existed.

[Domaine Bernard Gripa](../../../producers/domaine-bernard-gripa.md) adds an old Mauves line working both Saint-Joseph and Saint-Péray; [Domaine du Tunnel](../../../producers/domaine-du-tunnel.md) adds a later cross-appellation estate; [Domaine Laurent Fayolle](../../../producers/domaine-laurent-fayolle.md) reaches here from Gervans.

## A rule change in progress

A modification of the specification was adopted by the Comité national on 6 February 2025 and placed in the national opposition procedure. A proposal in opposition is not law. CARTA records it as a dated observation and keeps it out of the rules in force until homologation is confirmed.
