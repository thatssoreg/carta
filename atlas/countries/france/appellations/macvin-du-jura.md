# Macvin du Jura AOP

**A Jura liqueur wine, not a still-wine origin shorthand**

Macvin du Jura is a white, red, or rosé liqueur wine produced by combining grape must with Jura marc spirit under its own AOP. It belongs to the same regional wine world as Arbois or Côtes du Jura but governs a different category.

## Why it matters

Macvin makes the distinction between place and process concrete. The mapped regulatory area may overlap geographic AOPs, yet the bottle’s legal identity turns on the mistelle framework. It should not be confused with vin jaune, vin de paille, or ordinary fortified-wine generalizations.

## Grapes and colors

Jura’s five principal grapes provide the regional material context, while the current Macvin specification governs what is eligible for each color. CARTA keeps the high-level white/red/rosé fact sourced and leaves detailed blend rules to the authoritative specification.

## What the map shows
