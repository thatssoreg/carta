# Cornas AOP

**One commune, one colour, one grape — and the corridor's sharpest legal trap**

Cornas was recognised by the decree of 5 August 1938. Its specification states simply that *les vins sont issus du cépage syrah N*: red only, [Syrah](../../../grapes/syrah.md) only, with no accessory or white variety permitted at all. The specification in force is the one homologated by the arrêté of 4 July 2024.

## In Cornas is not Cornas

The appellation is delimited within the sole commune of Cornas — and a delimited area inside a commune is not the whole commune. [Guillaume Gilles](../../../producers/guillaume-gilles.md) bottles Les Peyrouses, an old-vine Syrah from the flats in that commune, as Côtes du Rhône. CARTA records the physical locality, the legal designation and the fact that it does not know where the parcel sits against the delimited boundary, and refuses to close that gap by guessing.

## The amphitheatre

The specification describes most of the vineyard on soils weathered from primary eruptive rock of the granite family — the porphyritic granites of Tournon — in an amphitheatre open to the south and sheltered from the north by the Arlettes massif, terraced behind stone walls. Granite continues physically south into [Saint-Péray](saint-peray.md), where the permitted colour and grapes change completely. The rock does not explain the rule.

## A transfer network, not a school

Cornas is the corridor's densest record of what actually passes between growers. Robert Michel leased [Guillaume Gilles](../../../producers/guillaume-gilles.md) a Chaillot parcel and made his cave available; he was also [Vincent Paris](../../../producers/domaine-vincent-paris.md)'s uncle, and Paris rented and then bought vines from him. [Thierry Allemand](../../../producers/thierry-allemand.md) worked for Joseph and then Robert Michel while helping [Noël Verset](../../../producers/domaine-noel-verset.md) at night, and bought old Reynard vines from him — and is described by the same source as "a disciple of no one's". [Franck Balthazar](../../../producers/domaine-franck-balthazar.md) is Verset's nephew and took a Chaillot parcel from him. [Domaine Auguste Clape](../../../producers/domaine-auguste-clape.md) stands beside all of it as an independent traditional reference.

Mentorship, employment, kinship, lease, cellar access and purchase are six different things. CARTA records them separately and invents no "Cornas school" to hold them.

## Who bottled first

The specification says the first bottling on behalf of a private cellar happened in 1952, fourteen years after recognition. One importer credits Joseph Michel with pioneering domaine-bottled Cornas in 1956; another credits Auguste Clape as the village's first producer to bottle his own wine in 1957. Three accounts, one unresolved question, all three kept.
