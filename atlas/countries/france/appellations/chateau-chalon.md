# Château-Chalon AOP

**A small geographic origin devoted to vin jaune**

Château-Chalon is the most tightly focused of Jura’s geographic AOPs. It governs vin jaune from Savagnin around a small set of villages and should not be used as a generic synonym for every Jura vin jaune.

## Why it matters

Its narrow legal identity makes three concepts easy to separate: Savagnin is the grape, vin jaune is the wine style, and Château-Chalon is a geographic appellation. Other Jura AOPs can support vin jaune under their own rules, but their names do not become Château-Chalon.

## What the map shows

The mapped shape is an official INAO regulatory geographic area, not planted vineyard. The small footprint becomes meaningful when compared directly with broad Côtes du Jura and multi-category Crémant du Jura or Macvin du Jura geography.

## Measured scale

The official CIVJ 2024 infographic reports Château-Chalon’s area on a 2023 revendication basis. CARTA keeps the year and scope with the number.
