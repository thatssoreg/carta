# Côtes du Jura AOP

**The broad geographic frame for Jura still wines**

Côtes du Jura is the region’s broad geographic appellation. Its current specification supports white, red, rosé, vin jaune, and vin de paille traditions across a long north–south area, making it the strongest counterexample to the idea that Jura means only oxidative white wine.

## Grapes and styles

Chardonnay and Savagnin are the principal white grapes. Pinot Noir, Poulsard, and Trousseau form the principal red-grape set. Vin jaune is made from Savagnin, but the appellation also accommodates topped-up whites, red wines, rosé, and concentrated vin de paille under their respective rules.

## What the map shows

The CARTA polygon is the INAO cartographic representation of the regulatory geographical area. It does not show approved parcels or actual planted vineyard. Compare it with the other Jura shapes to see how a broad geographic AOP can overlap a sparkling or fortified category.
