# Saint-Joseph AOP

**Sixty kilometres of different slopes under one name**

Saint-Joseph was recognised by the decree of 15 June 1956, the last of the eight crus to be created. Its geographic area covers 26 communes — 23 in the Ardèche and three in the Loire — strung over roughly sixty kilometres of right bank. The specification in force is the one homologated by the arrêté of 4 July 2024.

## Two colours, three grapes

Reds require [Syrah](../../../grapes/syrah.md) to be at least 90% of the holding's encépagement, with [Marsanne](../../../grapes/marsanne.md) and [Roussanne](../../../grapes/roussanne.md) permitted only as accessory varieties in mixed plantings inside the Syrah parcels, up to 10% of the vines; where both colours are used the wine must be [assembled at the grape stage](../../../practices/co-fermentation.md). Whites come from Marsanne and Roussanne, and the specification notes Marsanne at about 95% of white plantings.

## An outline that moved twice

Saint-Joseph began in 1956 on six communes around Tournon. It expanded in 1969 by twenty further communes, reaching north to the Condrieu area. Then parcel revisions approved on 9–10 February 1994 and 20 June 2018 cut the delimited parcel area from about 6,800 to about 3,400 hectares.

Today's outline is a made thing. The slopes did not move when the decree did.

## Three human clusters, no legal subregions

The historic core around Mauves, Tournon and Saint-Jean-de-Muzols carries [Domaine Pierre Gonon](../../../producers/domaine-gonon.md), [Domaine Bernard Gripa](../../../producers/domaine-bernard-gripa.md) and [Domaine Jean-Louis Chave](../../../producers/domaine-jean-louis-chave.md). The northern sector around Chavanay carries [Yves Cuilleron](../../../producers/domaine-yves-cuilleron.md) and [André Perret](../../../producers/domaine-andre-perret.md). And [Domaine Romaneaux-Destezet](../../../producers/romaneaux-destezet.md) is the corridor's sharpest spatial warning: Hervé Souhaut's base is at Arlebosc in the northern Ardèche, outside Saint-Joseph entirely, while some of his vines and wines are Saint-Joseph.

These are editorial groupings for reading a long appellation. They are not subregions, and CARTA does not invent any.
