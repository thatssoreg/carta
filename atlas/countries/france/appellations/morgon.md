# Morgon AOP

**A northern Beaujolais cru built around Gamay and named place**

Morgon is one of the ten Beaujolais crus. Its identity is geographic and legal: it is not a producer style, a synonym for all Beaujolais, or a guarantee that every wine will taste the same.

## How to read the map

The CARTA shape is INAO’s cartographic representation of Morgon’s regulatory geographic area. It is not planted-vineyard geometry. Compare it with Fleurie, Moulin-à-Vent, and the broad Beaujolais AOC to see how crus fit inside the larger regional world.

## Grape and site

Gamay noir à jus blanc is the central grape. Côte du Py is a named site within Morgon and an important local reference, but one site should not stand in for the whole appellation.
