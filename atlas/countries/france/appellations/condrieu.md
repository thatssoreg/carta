# Condrieu AOP

**One grape, one colour, and a vineyard that nearly vanished**

Condrieu was recognised by the decree of 27 April 1940 and runs across seven communes and three departments — Condrieu in the Rhône; Chavanay, Malleval, Saint-Michel-sur-Rhône, Saint-Pierre-de-Bœuf and Vérin in the Loire; Limony in the Ardèche. INAO gives the vineyard as roughly 100 hectares of terraced right-bank hillside. The specification in force is the one homologated by the arrêté of 4 July 2024.

## Viognier, and a sweetness range

The rule is one sentence: *les vins sont issus du cépage viognier B*. [Viognier](../../../grapes/viognier.md) and nothing else, as a still white wine.

"Dry white Viognier" is the usual case rather than the legal definition. The specification's own account describes a wine that was *doux* in the mid-nineteenth century and is now in most cases *sec*, sometimes *demi-sec*, with confidential quantities of *moelleux* or *doux* made from overripe grapes when conditions allow.

## Down to eight hectares, and back

The specification records phylloxera devastating the vineyard in 1893, then the First World War and industrial employment draining the labour the terraces needed, until by 1965 only some eight hectares remained. It records the vineyard climbing back past 140 hectares by 2010.

Trade accounts commonly give the low point as about seven hectares around 1960. CARTA keeps the specification's figure and date and records the divergence rather than splitting the difference.

## Who brought it back

The Syndicat des Vignerons de l'AOC Condrieu describes [Georges Vernay](../../../producers/domaine-georges-vernay.md) as the man who worked to rebuild the hillsides and rescue Viognier from obscurity — the "Pope of Condrieu". That is a documented leading role, and it is not the whole recovery: Francis Vernay had kept the family's foundational planting, [André Perret](../../../producers/domaine-andre-perret.md) returned in the 1980s specifically to plant and develop the AOC, [Yves Cuilleron](../../../producers/domaine-yves-cuilleron.md) took over 3.5 hectares in 1987 and became part of the next expansion, and [E. Guigal](../../../producers/e-guigal.md) put Condrieu in front of a much larger market.

[Château-Grillet](chateau-grillet.md) sits in the same landscape under its own appellation. Return to [Northern Rhône](../regions/northern-rhone.md).
