# Collines Rhodaniennes IGP

> **Navigation node:** this honest stub keeps a meaningful CARTA subject discoverable without presenting it as a finished baseline reference.

Collines Rhodaniennes is the protected geographical indication that carries much of the Rhône corridor's non-cru wine. It matters to [Northern Rhône](../regions/northern-rhone.md) for one reason: it is the legal home of fruit the eight crus do not cover, including the [Seyssuel](../../../producers/les-vins-de-vienne.md) vineyard replanted from 1996.

CARTA governs the category and deliberately leaves its mapped area as external INAO context in this release. Movement on the map between Seyssuel and Côte-Rôtie is tiny; movement in legal category is total.
