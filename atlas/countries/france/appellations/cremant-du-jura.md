# Crémant du Jura AOP

**Traditional-method sparkling wine across Jura**

Crémant du Jura governs sparkling wine made by the traditional method. Its geography overlaps the region’s still-wine origins, but its legal identity is based on a different wine category and production framework.

## Why the overlap matters

A vineyard can sit inside more than one relevant regulatory area because the areas answer different questions. A geographic still-wine AOP identifies one origin-and-style framework; Crémant du Jura identifies the sparkling framework. The overlap is information, not a cartographic error.

## Grapes and wine

The regional five-grape set supplies the broader context, with Chardonnay especially important. Exact current blend and aging requirements belong to the latest specification; CARTA does not infer them from regional custom.

## What the map shows

The polygon is INAO’s cartographic representation of the regulatory geographic area, not actual planted land. CIVJ’s 2024 key-figures sheet provides a separately scoped 2023 revendication-area measure.
