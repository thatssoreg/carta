# Arbois AOP

**The broadest single place-name entry into Jura wine**

Arbois is a geographic AOP in the Jura wine region and a strong first appellation for learning the region’s full range. It supports white, red, rosé, vin jaune, and vin de paille traditions rather than reducing Arbois to one grape or one cellar method.

## Place, grape, method

Chardonnay and Savagnin anchor the white-wine picture. Poulsard, Trousseau, and Pinot Noir anchor the red-wine picture. A label may then signal style or method—topped up, matured under voile, vin jaune, or vin de paille—but those terms do not replace the appellation identity.

## What the map shows

The mapped area is INAO’s cartographic representation of the regulatory geography. It is not a parcel map or a measure of planted vineyard. CIVJ’s separate 2023 revendication-basis area gives scale without changing what the polygon means.
