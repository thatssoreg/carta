# Jurançon

Jurançon is a French appellation in the Pyrenean foothills whose identity is closely tied to Petit Manseng and Gros Manseng. The current CARTA evidence describes hillside and terrace viticulture under oceanic influence, with a warm, dry southerly wind often important in autumn.

## Overview

Jurançon is historically associated with both sweet and dry white wine. INAO records sweet Jurançon recognition in 1936 and dry Jurançon recognition in 1975. Petit Manseng and Gros Manseng are described as the principal local grape varieties.

## Landscape & growing conditions

The accepted Run 01 record describes steep hillside and terrace viticulture in the Pyrenean foothills, oceanic influence, and hand harvesting linked to high-trained vines and difficult terrain.

## History & significance

Jurançon is important not only as a legal category but as a long-standing center of Manseng viticulture. Its combination of grape material, foothill conditions, and sweet/dry traditions makes it one of the key reference points for understanding Petit Manseng beyond a generic grape description.
