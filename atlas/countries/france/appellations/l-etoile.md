# L’Etoile AOP

**A small Jura origin centered on white-wine traditions**

L’Etoile is one of Jura’s four geographic wine AOPs. It is associated primarily with white wines, including vin jaune and vin de paille traditions, and sits at a very different scale from the broad Côtes du Jura frame.

## How to read it

The appellation name identifies legal origin; it does not specify one cellar method. Chardonnay and Savagnin provide the central grape context, while style terms explain how a particular wine was made or matured.

## What the map shows

The CARTA shape is the INAO representation of the regulatory geographic area. It should be compared with neighboring Jura appellations without treating overlap as contradiction: different legal wine categories can cover related territory.
