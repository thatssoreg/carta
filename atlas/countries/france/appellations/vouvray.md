# Vouvray AOP

**One Chenin origin, several stable style families**

Vouvray sits on the right bank of the Loire in Touraine and centers Chenin Blanc. Its legal and cultural importance comes partly from the range that one origin and grape can sustain: dry, demi-sec, moelleux, and sparkling wines.

## Avoid a common shortcut

Residual sweetness is a style distinction inside Vouvray, not a different geography. A dry Vouvray and a sweet Vouvray can share the appellation while expressing different harvest choices, concentration, and balance. Exact current thresholds belong to the specification and should not be reconstructed from generalized summaries.

## What the map shows

The polygon is INAO’s cartographic representation of the regulatory geographic area. It is not a map of Chenin parcels or actual plantings. On the wider Loire map, it provides one governed anchor within a much longer and more varied river-linked region.
