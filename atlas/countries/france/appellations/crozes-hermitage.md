# Crozes-Hermitage AOP

**The biggest cru, and the one that refuses a single description**

Crozes-Hermitage was recognised by the decree of 4 March 1937 and covers eleven Drôme communes on the left bank facing Tournon — Beaumont-Monteux, Chanos-Curson, Crozes-Hermitage, Erôme, Gervans, Larnage, Pont-de-l'Isère, La Roche-de-Glun, Serves-sur-Rhône, Tain-l'Hermitage and part of Mercurol-Veaunes. The specification in force is the one homologated by the arrêté of 10 December 2024.

## Two colours, three grapes

Reds require [Syrah](../../../grapes/syrah.md) to be at least 85% of the encépagement, with [Marsanne](../../../grapes/marsanne.md) and [Roussanne](../../../grapes/roussanne.md) admitted as accessory varieties in mixed plantings up to 15% of the vines; whites are Marsanne and Roussanne.

## Two grounds under one name

The specification splits the appellation itself. In the north the Rhône has cut down to the *granite de Tournon* between Crozes-Hermitage and Serves-sur-Rhône, and sandy clays and granitic arenas are held on the slopes by dry-stone terraces. In the south and east lie Quaternary terraces of alpine gravels, with loess locally at the Gervans quartier of Les Blancs and distinctive kaolin sands at Larnage. Les Chassis, the lowest and youngest terrace, forms a triangle whose apex is at Pont-de-l'Isère.

The same document notes that the northern granite is the formation exposed across the river at Tournon, in [Saint-Joseph](saint-joseph.md). Geology crosses the Rhône and the appellation boundary together; only one of them is a legal object.

## One commune becomes eleven

The 1937 decree limited the area to part of the single commune of Crozes-Hermitage. Postwar prosperity took it to eleven communes by 1952. A later reselection of the parcels grapes may come from cut production potential from 4,800 to 3,200 hectares — the specification's own narrative places that ten-year effort in the 1990s while dating its endorsement to June 1989, and CARTA records the inconsistency rather than choosing.

## People who make the contrast visible

[Domaine Alain Graillot](../../../producers/domaine-alain-graillot.md) makes the broad terrace country memorable; [Domaine Laurent Fayolle](../../../producers/domaine-laurent-fayolle.md), sixth generation at Gervans, farms the granite hills behind [Hermitage](hermitage.md); [Dard & Ribo](../../../producers/dard-et-ribo.md) assembled their holding by renting parcels across three appellations at once. Crozes-Hermitage is not blank space around a famous hill.
