# Béarn

Béarn here refers to the **historical and cultural territory** in southwestern France, not the legal Béarn appellation. That distinction matters because the older wine landscape, grape history, and present appellation system overlap without being identical.

## Overview

Run 01 uses Béarn to give historical depth to the western-Pyrenean grape landscape around Manseng, Courbu-family material, and Raffiat de Moncade. INAO historical material records a broader older white-grape mix, major vineyard contraction through disease and disruption, VDQS recognition in 1951, and AOC recognition in 1975.

## History & significance

The importance of Béarn in CARTA is not simply that an appellation exists with the same name. The older territory helps preserve continuity across grape material, neighboring wine zones, and historical wine culture that current appellation boundaries divide into separate legal systems.
