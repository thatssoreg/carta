# Jura

**A compact region where place, grape and method visibly overlap**

Jura is a narrow eastern French vineyard running along the western foothills of the Jura mountains. Its scale is part of its teaching power: within one compact region, a learner can compare geographic appellations, five principal grapes, oxidative and topped-up whites, vin jaune, traditional-method sparkling wine and a fortified mistelle without treating those categories as synonyms.

## Start with the map

The CARTA region label is an orientation point derived from the official INAO areas of the mapped Jura appellations. It is not a statutory Jura polygon. Select the individual appellation shapes to see the legal geography that actually underpins it.

The six regional wine AOPs belong to three useful families:

- [Arbois](../appellations/arbois.md), [Côtes du Jura](../appellations/cotes-du-jura.md), [L’Etoile](../appellations/l-etoile.md), and [Château-Chalon](../appellations/chateau-chalon.md) are geographic still-wine origins.
- [Crémant du Jura](../appellations/cremant-du-jura.md) governs traditional-method sparkling wine across overlapping regional geography.
- [Macvin du Jura](../appellations/macvin-du-jura.md) governs a liqueur wine; it is not a still-wine appellation or a synonym for vin jaune.

## The grape picture

[Chardonnay](../../../grapes/chardonnay.md) and [Savagnin](../../../grapes/savagnin.md) lead the planted mix. Poulsard (locally Ploussard), Pinot Noir, and Trousseau form the red-grape trio. The official 2024 CIVJ key-figures sheet gives each variety a measured share on its stated 2023 basis; CARTA keeps those values and that denominator in machine claims rather than inferring them from this prose.

## The style picture

Jura’s familiar vocabulary describes different axes:

- **Ouillé** and **sous voile** describe cellar approach, not appellations.
- **Vin jaune** is a legally governed wine style made from Savagnin; Château-Chalon is its most restrictive geographic AOP, while other Jura appellations can also provide a vin-jaune framework under their own rules.
- **Vin de paille** describes a concentrated sweet style governed within eligible geographic AOPs.
- **Crémant** is sparkling wine made by the traditional method.
- **Macvin** is a mistelle made by combining grape must and marc spirit under its own AOP.

Keeping these axes separate prevents the common mistake of turning one celebrated style into a definition of the whole region.

## Producers as paths, not rankings

[Domaine Labet](../../../producers/domaine-labet.md) is a useful path into Chardonnay, Savagnin, topped-up and sous-voile contrasts. [Domaine de la Tournelle](../../../producers/domaine-de-la-tournelle.md), [Maison Pierre Overnoy / Emmanuel Houillon](../../../producers/maison-pierre-overnoy.md), [Renaud Bruyère & Adeline Houillon](../../../producers/bruyere-houillon.md), and [Domaine de Saint Pierre](../../../producers/domaine-de-saint-pierre-jura.md) widen the current CARTA network. They are representative anchors, not a quality ranking or a complete regional list.
