# Loire Valley

**River continuity, not sameness**

CARTA treats the Loire Valley as an analytical wine corridor, not a single vineyard, appellation, institutional territory, or regulatory polygon. Its governing question is: **How can one river connect Muscadet, Savennières, Vouvray, Chinon, and Sancerre without making them versions of the same place?**

The answer begins with movement. The Loire supplies continuity, while tributaries, geology, grapes, legal origins, wine forms, and human histories keep changing. Its human equivalent is a set of overlapping lineages—not one “Loire natural-wine school.”

## Place: a corridor and its branches

Read the world through six analytical components:

- **Pays Nantais:** the Atlantic opening through Muscadet, Muscadet Sèvre et Maine, and Melon de Bourgogne.
- **Anjou:** a hinge of schist country, the Layon, dry and sweet Chenin contexts, and classification-friction routes.
- **Saumur:** tuffeau country, Saumur and Saumur-Champigny, Cabernet Franc and Chenin, still and sparkling forms.
- **Touraine:** Vouvray, Montlouis, Chinon, Cheverny, and Cour-Cheverny as a diversity engine rather than a neutral midpoint.
- **Vallée du Loir:** a northern tributary branch through Jasnières and Coteaux du Loir. The Loir is not the Loire.
- **Centre-Loire:** an eastern reset, with its own interprofessional body and distinct Sancerre, Pouilly-Fumé, and Pouilly-sur-Loire origins.

The physical sequence runs broadly from the crystalline Armorican context of the Nantais through Anjou schist and Saumur–Touraine tuffeau/chalk toward Centre-Loire’s sedimentary hills. This is orientation, never geology-to-flavour causality. CARTA renders only two small Loire terrain proofs—Savennières/Layon and Sancerre/Pouilly—and does not fill the corridor with invented relief.

The map’s Loire label is a derived orientation point from governed child appellations. It is not a Loire Valley boundary.

## Rules: five objects that must stay separate

Current AOP regulatory areas, Val de Loire IGP, InterLoire’s operating scope, the Bureau Interprofessionnel des Vins du Centre-Loire’s scope, and UNESCO’s Loire cultural landscape are not interchangeable. CARTA’s analytical corridor is a sixth object. Each answers a different question.

This is why Saumur can remain distinct from Saumur-Champigny, why Savennières, Savennières Roche aux Moines, and Coulée de Serrant keep separate mapped identities, and why Centre-Loire is not silently folded into an InterLoire denominator.

Vin de France is handled with the same precision: it is a legal classification, not a movement badge. Ask which wine, vintage, rule, control event, or producer decision is involved. A label cannot, by itself, prove farming or cellar practice.

## Grapes and wine forms

Four broad routes make the corridor legible without exhausting it: Melon de Bourgogne in the western Muscadet world; Chenin across dry, sweet, and sparkling contexts; Cabernet Franc through central red-wine origins; and Sauvignon as an eastern route into Centre-Loire. Pineau d’Aunis, Grolleau, Gamay, Côt, Romorantin, Folle Blanche, Menu Pineau, and other grapes keep the story plural. Their presence never implies universal permission across the region.

## People: five doors, then an ecology

Five producer doors organize the first passage through the world:

1. [Domaines Landron](../../../producers/domaines-landron.md) — dated farming change and continuity in the Pays Nantais.
2. [La Coulée de Serrant / Joly](../../../producers/coulee-de-serrant.md) — estate biodynamic chronology and the later Renaissance des Appellations network.
3. [Clos Rougeard / the Foucault world](../../../producers/clos-rougeard.md) — family history, ownership change, Cabernet Franc and Chenin, and a work lineage toward Domaine du Collier.
4. [Clos du Tue-Boeuf / Puzelat](../../../producers/clos-du-tue-boeuf.md) — Touraine, grape and classification plurality, and the Bonhomme work/collaboration line.
5. [Domaine Alexandre Bain](../../../producers/domaine-alexandre-bain.md) — an evidence-bounded Pouilly-Fumé control dispute that corrects a durable myth.

[Domaine Huet](../../../producers/domaine-huet.md) stands immediately behind those five as a deep Vouvray and Chenin route. Mosse family succession, Bellivière’s handoff, the Breton family chronology, Domaine du Collier, Richard Leroy, Pierre-Olivier Bonhomme, and other governed nodes widen the ecology. They do not become members of one school merely because routes overlap.

## Evidence boundary

InterLoire’s published hectares and grape shares remain attached to InterLoire’s stated denominator; they are not totals for every Loire vineyard. Producer markers are bases or municipality/locality orientations, not holdings. INAO shapes are regulatory-area representations, not planted-parcel maps. Current legal detail still belongs in each cahier des charges, and bottle-level classification changes remain deliberately deferred until wine- and vintage-specific evidence exists.
