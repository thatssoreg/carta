# Béarn

**CARTA ID:** `place:bearn`  
**Type:** place  
**Place kind:** historical territory  
**Status:** provisional

## What this place means in CARTA

This page represents **historical/cultural Béarn**, not the legal Béarn appellation. Run 01 showed that those two meanings are related but not interchangeable.

## Why it matters

Béarn provides historical depth to the western-Pyrenean grape landscape and helps explain why Manseng, Courbu-family material, and Raffiat de Moncade cannot be understood only through current appellation boxes.
