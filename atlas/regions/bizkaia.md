# Bizkaia

**CARTA ID:** `place:bizkaia`  
**Type:** place  
**Place kind:** administrative region  
**Status:** active

## What this place means in CARTA

Bizkaia is the administrative geography containing Balmaseda and the legal landscape of Bizkaiko Txakolina represented in Run 01. CARTA keeps it distinct from the broader and more ambiguous cultural idea of the Basque Country.
