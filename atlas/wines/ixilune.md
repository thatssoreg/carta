# Ixilune

**CARTA ID:** `wine:ixilune`  
**Type:** wine

## Why I should care

Ixilune makes the grape network tangible by bringing Raffiat de Moncade, Petit Manseng, Petit Courbu, and sometimes Gros Manseng into a single cuvée lineage.
