# Roussanne

**Legally paired with Marsanne, and not the same thing**

Roussanne is named alongside [Marsanne](marsanne.md) in every white rule of the Northern Rhône — [Saint-Joseph](../countries/france/appellations/saint-joseph.md), [Crozes-Hermitage](../countries/france/appellations/crozes-hermitage.md), [Hermitage](../countries/france/appellations/hermitage.md), the [Hermitage vin de paille mention](../classifications/hermitage-vin-de-paille.md) and [Saint-Péray](../countries/france/appellations/saint-peray.md) — and as an accessory variety in three of the red rules.

That pairing is a legal fact and nothing more. Where CARTA has a planting figure it points the other way: the Saint-Joseph specification puts Marsanne at about 95% of that appellation's white plantings, which leaves Roussanne a small share of a legally equal position.

## What is deliberately missing

CARTA holds no parentage, synonymy or dated planting series for Roussanne. Those wait for primary grape authority and dated statistics rather than being inferred from the appellation rules that name it.
