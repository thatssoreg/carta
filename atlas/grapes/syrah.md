# Syrah

**One red grape, and a different legal weight at every boundary**

Syrah is the only red variety permitted in any of the Northern Rhône's five red crus, and the corridor is where its legal identity is most visible. Its weight changes at every line on the map: sole variety in [Cornas](../countries/france/appellations/cornas.md), at least 90% of the encépagement in [Saint-Joseph](../countries/france/appellations/saint-joseph.md), at least 85% in [Crozes-Hermitage](../countries/france/appellations/crozes-hermitage.md) and [Hermitage](../countries/france/appellations/hermitage.md), and at least 80% in [Côte-Rôtie](../countries/france/appellations/cote-rotie.md), where a white grape may join it.

## Sérine is vocabulary, not a second grape

Importer and producer material around Côte-Rôtie regularly uses *Sérine* for the local selection of Syrah — [Domaine Christiane Chambeyron-Manin](../producers/domaine-christiane-chambeyron-manin.md)'s Côte Brune parcel is described that way, and [Jean-Michel Stéphan](../producers/jean-michel-stephan.md) replants by massale selection of Sérine. CARTA holds Sérine as local selection and naming vocabulary attached to Syrah. It is not a separate grape identity and not a universal synonym.

## Where the grape comes from

CARTA governs one ampelographic relationship for Syrah: [Dureza](dureza.md) is recorded as a parent. Anything further — the second parent, the wider family, the Syrah/Shiraz naming split — waits for primary grape authority rather than being assembled from trade prose.

## Whole cluster is not a regional signature

The Northern Rhône is often summarised as whole-bunch Syrah. The evidence CARTA holds says something more useful: [Guillaume Gilles](../producers/guillaume-gilles.md) eschews the destemmer in virtually all cases, [Dard & Ribo](../producers/dard-et-ribo.md) normally use whole bunches but destem when sunburn requires sorting, [Domaine Auguste Clape](../producers/domaine-auguste-clape.md)'s old-vine fruit is not destemmed, and [Domaine Jamet](../producers/domaine-jamet.md) ferments mostly whole-cluster across a mosaic of plots. Those are four different implementations, not one regional habit.

Read the grape through [Northern Rhône](../countries/france/regions/northern-rhone.md).
