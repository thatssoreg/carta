# Melon de Bourgogne

Melon de Bourgogne is the defining grape of the Muscadet wine world at the Atlantic end of the Loire Valley. Its name can mislead a learner into placing its central modern identity in Burgundy; CARTA keeps grape name, current regional role, and appellation identity separate.

## Loire role

The official InterLoire guide reports Melon’s share of the planted grape area within its stated 2023 departmental scope. That regional measure is context, not a replacement for the legal rules of Muscadet or its geographic denominations.

## Why it matters

Melon gives the western Loire a distinct material center and makes the region’s west-to-east change visible. It belongs beside Chenin Blanc, Cabernet Franc, and Sauvignon in a Loire overview precisely because no one of those grapes defines the entire valley.
