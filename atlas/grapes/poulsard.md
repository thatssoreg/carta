# Poulsard / Ploussard

Poulsard is one of Jura’s three principal red grapes. The local name **Ploussard** is especially visible around Arbois; CARTA treats it as an alternate name for the same governed grape identity rather than a second variety.

## Regional role

Poulsard belongs to the red and rosé framework of Jura geographic appellations and can also contribute within other regional wine categories under their current rules. The official CIVJ 2024 key-figures sheet reports its share together with the Ploussard name on a 2023 basis.

## Why it matters

The grape is a useful reminder that color intensity, tannin, and grape identity are different questions. Jura’s red-wine story cannot be reduced to Pinot Noir, and a pale red color does not make Poulsard a rosé grape category.
