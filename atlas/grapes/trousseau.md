# Trousseau

Trousseau is one of Jura’s three principal red grapes, alongside Poulsard and Pinot Noir. It occupies a smaller share than either Chardonnay or Savagnin in the official regional planting picture but remains essential to understanding Jura red wine.

## Regional role

Trousseau belongs to the red and rosé framework of the geographic Jura appellations and can participate in other regional wine categories under their current rules. Exact eligibility belongs to the relevant specification rather than a generic grape summary.

## Why it matters

Trousseau widens the learner’s frame beyond the region’s celebrated whites and oxidative traditions. Its presence helps explain why Jura must be read as a five-grape wine world rather than a Savagnin monoculture.
