# Raffiat de Moncade

Raffiat de Moncade is a white grape associated with the Béarn/western-Pyrenean world. Run 01 turned it from an obscure supporting name into one of the pilot's most interesting ampelographic threads because its reported lineage reaches backward to probable parentage and forward into twentieth-century breeding.

## Genetics & lineage

Plantgrape reports Raffiat de Moncade as **probably resulting from a cross between Gouais blanc and Bouchalès**. CARTA preserves that as proposed/probable parentage rather than stronger certainty.

Raffiat de Moncade later became a confirmed parent of **Arriloba**, obtained by INRA in 1954 through a cross with Sauvignon.

## Where it matters

The current corpus places Raffiat de Moncade in the older Béarn white-grape landscape and in contemporary wine through Imanol Garay's Ixilune.

A full baseline still needs to establish its present planting geography, rarity, historical significance, viticultural behavior, and whether meaningful pockets survive beyond the immediate Run 01 ecosystem.
