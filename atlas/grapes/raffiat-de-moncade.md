# Raffiat de Moncade

Raffiat de Moncade is a rare white grape associated with southwest France and the Béarn/Pyrenean grape world. CARTA knows enough to place it in the graph, but **not yet enough to publish a generous baseline grape reference**.

## What is established

Plantgrape reports Raffiat de Moncade as probably resulting from a cross between **Gouais blanc** and **Bouchalès**. That parentage is represented as proposed rather than absolute.

Raffiat de Moncade is also a documented parent of **Arriloba**, an INRA crossing made with Sauvignon.

The Run 01 ecosystem connected Raffiat to [Imanol Garay](../producers/imanol-garay.md) through documented vintages of [Ixilune](../wines/ixilune.md). That is enough to show that the grape is not merely a historical catalogue entry.

## What is not yet established well enough

Run 02 supplied a full sensory profile, precise yield/disease claims, current varietal bottlings, legal-appellation statements, and a producer list. Too much of that material could not be independently tied to strong grape-specific sources.

CARTA is therefore withholding:

- a definitive “in the glass” profile
- a current acreage estimate
- a confident modern producer canon
- claims that the grape is currently permitted in specific appellations unless verified from current law
- claims that a particular contemporary bottle is 100% Raffiat without wine-specific evidence

## Why the grape still matters

Raffiat de Moncade is exactly the kind of grape CARTA should keep visible even when the reference is incomplete. It sits inside a dense regional web of old grape material, crossing history, modern low-intervention producers, and genetics.

Its rarity is not itself proof of importance. Its relationships are what earn it a place in the system.

## Genetics

- proposed parents: Gouais blanc × Bouchalès
- documented offspring: Arriloba, with Sauvignon as the other parent

## Where it matters

The current evidence places the grape in the southwest-French / Béarn context. A deeper regional and legal mapping is still required.
