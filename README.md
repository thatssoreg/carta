# CARTA

**Cartography · Ampelography · Relationships · Time · Access**

CARTA is a spatial-temporal wine knowledge and field-intelligence system. It treats wine as a connected world of places, grapes, producers, people, vineyards, wines, law, practices, and time, with evidence attached to the relationships that make those things intelligible.

Its underlying architecture is **STRATA v0.2: Space · Time · Relationships · Appellations · Terroir · Ampelography**.

**Start with the [CARTA Human Reference](atlas/README.md).** It is the reader-facing way into the system.

## Why CARTA exists

Most wine references are excellent at isolated entries. CARTA is built for the questions that appear between them:

- What sits inside this appellation, and what contains it?
- Which grapes connect these places, and when are similar names actually different identities?
- How is a producer connected to a vineyard, collaborator, practice, wine, or legal category?
- How can one persistent wine identity carry different vintage-specific or multi-vintage facts through time?
- Which nearby or related profiles are worth following next?
- What evidence supports a relationship, and what has CARTA deliberately refused to guess?

The point is not to flatten wine into a giant graph. The point is to preserve the distinctions that make movement through wine meaningful: biological identity, legal naming, geography, professional relationships, production, classification, time, and evidence.

## Explore CARTA

A few existing doors show different parts of the system at work:

- **[Pyrenean Atlantic](atlas/ecosystems/pyrenean-atlantic.md)** shows an ecosystem generated by overlapping wine-law geography, grape naming, producer mobility, collaboration, and access rather than by a single political boundary.
- **[Hiyu Wine Farm](atlas/producers/hiyu-wine-farm.md)** shows a composite producer world and a persistent wine identity whose multi-vintage production history changes through time without becoming a new wine entity every vintage.
- **[St. Helena AVA](atlas/countries/united-states/appellations/st-helena-ava.md)** and **[Napa Valley AVA](atlas/countries/united-states/appellations/napa-valley-ava.md)** show evidence-backed geographic containment and CARTA's refusal to invent missing geography.
- **[Petit Manseng](atlas/grapes/petit-manseng.md)** is a grape entry point into ampelography, jurisdictional naming, place, producers, and related profiles.
- **[Jurançon](atlas/countries/france/appellations/jurancon.md)** shows legal wine geography meeting grape tradition, history, and surrounding regional context.
- **[Domaine Labet](atlas/producers/domaine-labet.md)** shows an honest navigation stub: useful, sourced machine authority can be discoverable before CARTA pretends a full producer dossier exists.

## How CARTA works

CARTA has one knowledge authority and one reader-facing projection:

**evidence → governed machine authority → deterministic Human Reference**

The machine authority lives in [`data/`](data/) as structured entities, relationships, claims, sources, names, spatial assertions, and profile dispositions. The Human Reference lives in [`atlas/`](atlas/) and turns that governed authority into readable profiles, indexes, provenance, and connected paths.

Human Reference is not independently authored truth. Generated navigation, indexes, and provenance come from governed records. If structured authority and prose disagree, the machine layer wins until the discrepancy is resolved editorially.

Personal Lens remains external and private. Personal taste, holdings, hypotheses, watchlists, and production questions can reference stable CARTA IDs without becoming shareable Reference authority.

## What makes CARTA trustworthy

CARTA is designed to be explicit about both knowledge and uncertainty.

- Claims carry evidence and source fitness rather than borrowing authority from proximity.
- Temporal facts remain temporal. CARTA does not turn a dated classification, practice, or availability observation into a timeless statement.
- Geography is authored from evidence. Missing containment and geometry are not guessed to make navigation look complete.
- Persistent wines remain durable identities while dated manifestations and production histories attach through claims and relationships.
- Human Reference generation is deterministic and validated against the machine layer.
- Navigation uses governed relationships, structural routes, editorial anchors, and kind-aware two-hop semantics without profile-specific exceptions.
- Validation checks schema integrity, cross-references, profile governance, generated freshness, local Human Reference links, and reachability.

## Current state

CARTA is an active STRATA v0.2 reference system with governed machine authority and a deterministic Human Reference spanning countries, regions, appellations, landscapes, ecosystems, producers, people, grapes, wines, classifications, and related kinds. Exact record counts are reported by `python scripts/validate_data.py` rather than copied into this README, so the repository does not maintain a second status ledger.

The architecture, authority rules, persistent wine identity model, profile kinds, 16-link navigation cap, and Human Reference generation contract are stable. New knowledge can expand the system without reopening those foundations.

## Relationship to Natural Wine 2.5

Natural Wine 2.5 is deliberately edited and compressed for teaching. CARTA is the expansive reference and relationship system beside it. The curriculum can stay selective while CARTA preserves the deeper spatial, temporal, evidentiary, and relational world behind the lesson.
